# Start here

## Before the workshop

Have a Google account and a laptop with a browser. Download and extract the repository.

## During the workshop

1. Open [the student lesson](lessons/Session1_Student.html) locally in your browser.
2. Download [the main notebook](notebooks/session1.ipynb).
3. In [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/), choose **File → Upload notebook**, then select `session1.ipynb`.
4. Save your own copy in Drive and choose **Runtime → Change runtime type → T4 GPU**.
5. Follow the notebook's setup cells, then continue in order. After a runtime restart, follow its instructions for rerunning setup and imports.

Only the notebook needs to be uploaded to Colab. The interactive lesson and [presentation slides](slides/Slides.pptx) stay on your computer.

## Optional follow-up resources

- [Standalone labs](labs/README.md): training and serving examples for your own GPU or HPC environment.

Use the notebook's installation instructions in Colab. The standalone requirements file is for local or HPC training, and vLLM belongs in a separate serving environment.
